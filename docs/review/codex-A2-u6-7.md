General suggestions

- Add explicit facts whenever two connectors or quantity expressions can create different but grammatical meanings.
- Keep listening distractors clearly distinct across several sounds, not merely by a final sound or short unstressed word.
- Make every role-play opening naturally elicit at least one listed target phrase in the learner’s first response.
