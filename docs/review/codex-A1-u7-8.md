General suggestions

- Make subjective situations—especially price judgments—state the learner’s opinion or budget explicitly.
- Avoid near-minimal sound contrasts in listening distractors unless pronunciation discrimination is the stated objective.
- Ensure role-play openings naturally prompt the current lesson’s language, rather than a yes/no answer or grammar from an earlier lesson.
- Teach singular and plural versions explicitly before assessing both.
