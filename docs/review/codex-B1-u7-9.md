### General suggestions

- Recheck every “Tap the wrong word” item by attempting to repair a different word, especially in agreement and relative-clause sentences.
- Give explicit evidence or certainty cues whenever **must**, **might**, and **can’t** appear as competing answers.
- Ensure role-play openings elicit the taught phrase in the same tense, and give both members of paired forms such as **which/that** positive practice.
