### General suggestions

- Most distractors test elementary agreement or word-form errors rather than C1 discourse choices. Add more grammatical but pragmatically wrong alternatives so learners must distinguish logical force, stance, register, and rhetorical function.
- Avoid using dialect-valid forms as supposedly impossible distractors, particularly in lessons explicitly teaching informal spoken English.
- Several “academic” role plays are oral grammar questions rather than role plays. Give the tutor a concrete claim, misunderstanding, or communicative constraint that naturally prompts the target phrase.
- For *it follows that*, state every premise needed to make the conclusion genuinely follow. Otherwise the exercise teaches confidence rather than valid reasoning.
