General suggestions

- Add explicit situational meaning when several conjunctions would otherwise make grammatical sentences.
- For every “Tap the wrong word,” test whether changing any other single word also repairs the sentence.
- For arrange exercises, try both reordering and substituting the extra tile while omitting target tiles.
- Teach contractions and special forms explicitly before requiring them in role play.
- Keep role-play openings concrete, simple, and shaped to elicit a target phrase in the learner’s first response.
