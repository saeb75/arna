### General suggestions

- Test every “Tap the wrong word” item by trying to repair each individual word.
- Avoid phoneme-level minimal pairs in listening distractors.
- State the decisive fact or opinion explicitly whenever two otherwise natural responses are possible.
- Model every grammatical form before testing it.
