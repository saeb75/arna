### General suggestions

- Run every arrange-item tile set through an alternative-order check and accept every natural order or redesign the tiles.
- Do not use distractors that form another valid collocation or sentence without enough context to disambiguate it.
- Make role-play openings elicit target language from the learner rather than saying the target for them.
- Keep listening distractors distinct in multiple stressed words, not chiefly in contractions or short function-word sequences.
