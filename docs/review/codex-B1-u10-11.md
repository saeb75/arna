Add a teaching point and at least three `however` items, or rename the lesson and focus to match `although`, `even though`, and `despite that`. |

| 61 — `b1-explaining-yourself-clearly` | “I still don’t understand how plants make food from sunlight. ### General suggestions

- In meaning-change lessons, practise both constructions as correct answers and describe their meanings as tendencies where native usage overlaps.
- Test every distractor by inserting it literally and checking both grammaticality and truth in context.
- Keep role plays grounded in information supplied by the opening and in familiar knowledge, so they test the target language rather than outside expertise.
