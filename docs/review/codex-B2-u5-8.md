General suggestions

- Mechanically test every arrange item for alternative grammatical permutations before release.
- For “Tap the wrong word,” verify that changing any other single word cannot repair the sentence.
- Give fill-gap distractors enough context to exclude valid alternative meanings.
- Make each role-play opening establish the discourse condition required by its target phrase, rather than merely naming the general topic.
