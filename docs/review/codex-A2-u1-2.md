### General suggestions

- Check distractors as complete sentences for alternative meanings, not only against the target grammar.
- Keep time-expression items correct regardless of the date on which learners see them.
- Make every voice-only opening self-contained; avoid instructions to look at or show something.
- Keep non-target vocabulary at A2 and model standard punctuation consistently.
