General suggestions:

- Replace the many elementary malformed distractors—such as “I’d weighs,” “our position are,” and “what I’d bringing”—with grammatical but pragmatically wrong alternatives. Those errors are not plausible C2 distractors and make the intended answer mechanically obvious.
- Qualify discourse claims with “often,” “can,” or “when context has not already supplied…” Many current rules are contradicted by their own standalone examples.
- Give open opinion items enough facts to make the intended pragmatic move uniquely appropriate.
- Where meaning depends on warmth, irony, or sincerity, test contrasting audio or specify delivery; ordinary speech recognition cannot validate the distinction.
- Ensure voice-only openings name every shared referent and make the learner’s first natural response contain a target phrase.
